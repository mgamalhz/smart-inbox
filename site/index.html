Questions owners usually ask

Does it reply to my customers on its own?

Never. It drafts; you read, edit and press send.

Where does my data go?

Only the message text, to the provider you choose — Anthropic, OpenAI or Gemini. Names, emails and phone numbers are stripped first.

What if it labels something wrong?

Every item has "Wrong label?" — corrections feed back. Drafts are proposals, never actions.

Do I need to replace my inbox or retrain my team?

No. It layers on your existing inbox. Switch it off and everything is exactly as before.

Is there a subscription?

No. The skill is free forever. Done-for-you installation is a one-off fee, agreed on the call before work starts.

My system isn't Laravel — can you still help?

It's built for Laravel apps. On the call we'll look at what you run — if it's not a fit, I'll say so.
